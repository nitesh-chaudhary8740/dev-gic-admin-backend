Welcome to GIC Re

The entire general insurance business in India was nationalised by the General Insurance Business (Nationalisation) Act, 1972 (GIBNA). General Insurance Corporation of India (GIC) was formed under Section 9(1) of GIBNA and incorporated on 22 November 1972, for the purpose of superintending, controlling and carrying on the business of general insurance.
